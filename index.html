Henrik Bredahl Hansen

Software Developer

Curious by nature, a problem-solver at heart.

I’m Henrik, a software developer who enjoys understanding how things work and turning that knowledge into useful software.

After graduating in Game Development and Learning Technology from the University of Southern Denmark, I’m now looking for a place where I can apply my skills, continue to grow, and help build something meaningful.

Problem-solving has fascinated me from an early age. Whether through Rubik’s cubes, physical puzzles, video games, or, eventually, programming. That curiosity still shapes how I approach my work today. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar technologies, breaking complex problems into manageable pieces, and following an idea all the way from an early experiment to a working solution.

As a colleague, I value openness, reliability, and sharing knowledge. I’m not afraid to ask questions when something is unclear, and I enjoy learning from the people around me while contributing my own perspective and experience.

Away from the keyboard, you’ll often find me bouldering, training for a triathlon, playing the piano, reading, or getting far too invested in a board game.
